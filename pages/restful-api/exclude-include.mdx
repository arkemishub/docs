## Include & Exclude

<RestfulApiContainer>
    <div>
        The "Include & Exclude" params are the simplest way to exclude or include items in your GET all responses. The
        most common use is when you want a light response, ex. in a `struct` response.

        #### Params
        <RestfulApiTable
            columns={BODY_COLUMNS}
            rows={[
                {key: 'include', value: 'string[]' },
                {key: 'exclude', value: 'string[]' },
            ]}
        />

        Use a list of string (id of parameters) to return or not the item in the response.
        Looks on right responses example how the initial data change with include & exclude parameter
    </div>
    <div>
        <RestfulApiRequest
